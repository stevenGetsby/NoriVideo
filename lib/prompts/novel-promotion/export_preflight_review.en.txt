- Do not duplicate the same problem. Group it under the workflow stage that gives the production team the clearest fix.
- Sort issues by production priority: blocker before warning before info, then script/assets/storyboard/shots/voice/export dependency order. next_actions must follow the same priority order.

Workflow surfaces to review:
1. Script parsing: whether episode, scene, source_text/source_anchor, summary, info_points, reasoning, and scene visual style can trace back to source material.
2. Asset setup: whether major characters, key props, environments, and voices have reusable descriptions, prompt/base_prompt, variants, coverage_scenes/coverage_episodes, confirmation/production status, or reference material.
3. Storyboard structure: whether panel_id, panel_number, description, source_text/source_anchor, referenced_assets, characters, location, props, continuity_notes, and voice_refs can support shot production.
4. Shot prompts: whether image_prompt/visual_prompt/imagePrompt, description, and video_prompt all support each other, and whether shot_type, camera_move, duration, visual_style, and visual_style_description are usable for video generation.
5. Media delivery: whether each exportable panel has image/video URLs or completed status, and whether audio/dubbing assets bind to dialogue, voice_refs, or panels.
6. Manifest delivery: whether exported panel rows preserve source text, still-frame prompt evidence, video prompt evidence, media URLs, duration, and traceable asset references.
7. Continuity: whether character appearance, environment, prop state, action handoff, narration, dialogue ownership, and prompt-to-media consistency have obvious breaks.
