Rules:
1. Keep panel order unchanged.
2. Keep source_text semantically aligned with input; do not rewrite story meaning.
3. video_prompt should be motion-ready and concrete.
4. For human actors, prefer age+gender wording in video_prompt when naming camera subjects. For animals, fairy-tale creatures, objects, or other non-human subjects, keep the species/object label such as "little rabbit", "firefly", or "moon lamp"; never rewrite them as boys, girls, men, or women unless the story explicitly makes them human.
5. Preserve and refine `props`; include only visible story props that are held, used, or changing state in that panel. Use [] when no prop appears.
6. For ordinary stories and fairy tales, do not put theme, emotion, moral lesson, kindness, or selling points into `props`; only visible entities such as a leaf, moon lamp, puddle, key, or letter are valid props.
7. Only product, brand, platform, or ad prompts may treat props as merchandise or commercial display objects.
8. Preserve every input `slot` when the character is stably positioned, and reflect it as a preferred anchor in refined description/video_prompt.
9. `slot` is not an absolute boundary. If the shot is clearly about movement, entry/exit, path traversal, transition space, temporary space, empty space, imagination, dream, memory, or abstract/non-literal space, you may remove `slot` or keep it without forcing a rigid static match.
10. When no `slot` is used, decide placement freely from source text, action flow, spatial logic, and cinematic staging.
11. Do not shorten, rewrite, summarize, or replace a provided `slot` phrase with a short token.
12. Preserve or refine `visual_style` and `visual_style_description` from the input panels. They may describe only art direction, light, color, texture, composition, or mood, and must not add story facts.
13. video_prompt must explicitly state the subject action, camera movement, and referenced scene/asset. If dialogue is present, state that the speaker is speaking. If a prop changes state, state that visible change.
14. `video_prompt` must follow this production grammar: visual subject + visible action + referenced location/prop/character asset + camera movement + start/end state of the beat + continuity constraint from the previous or next panel.
15. Do not use generic video wording such as "cinematic shot" or "dramatic movement" by itself. The prompt must say who or what moves, what changes on screen, where it happens, and how the camera moves.
16. If dialogue or voiceover belongs to the panel, `video_prompt` must name the visible speaker or reaction subject and include lip-sync preparation such as "speaking" or "reacting silently"; never add subtitles or visible text.
17. duration must fit the video_prompt complexity and cannot be the same value for every panel by default.
18. Preserve `panel_id`, `source_anchor`, `referenced_assets`, `continuity_notes`, and `voice_refs` from input whenever present. Refine only when the visual detail changes the panel's visible asset set or voice match.
19. If missing, generate `source_anchor` from source_text, `referenced_assets` from the final visible characters/location/props, and `voice_refs` from dialogue or voiceover in source_text.
20. `image_prompt` must be a still-frame prompt aligned with description, visual_style, referenced_assets, and location. It must not include motion-only instructions.
21. `visual_prompt` is the FrameOS-compatible alias for the still-frame prompt. Preserve it from input when present; otherwise mirror `image_prompt`.
22. Return JSON array only.
